Linux is a free and open-source operating system created by Linus Torvalds in 1991. It was inspired by the Unix operating system and built as a hobby project that eventually evolved into a powerful and widely used OS.

The GNU/Linux philosophy emphasizes freedom, collaboration, and community-driven development. It adheres to the principles of the GNU Project, which promotes software that can be freely used, modified, and distributed.

Linux follows the Unix philosophy of "doing one thing and doing it well," encouraging modular design and the use of simple, composable tools.
